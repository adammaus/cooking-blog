_An easy dinner for a weekend night._
*Makes 5-8 rolls*

## Ingredients
* 2 tbsp sesame oil, divided
* 1 tbsp minced peeled fresh ginger
* 4 garlic cloves, sliced
* 1 cup carrots, cut into match-sticks
* 8oz mushrooms, sliced
* 1 cup (1-inch) green onion pieces
* 4 cups napa cabbage, thinly sliced
* 1/4 tsp salt
* 1 tsp sugar
* 1 package extra firm tofu, drained and cubed
* 2 tbsp hoisin sauce

## Directions
1. Heat a large skillet with 1 tbsp sesame oil over medium heat.
1. Add ginger and garlic and cook for 30 seconds, stirring constantly.
1. Increase heat to medium-high.
1. Add carrots and mushrooms. Cook 2 minutes, stirring frequently.
1. Add green onions and napa cabbage. Cook 2 minutes, stirring frequently.
1. Put cabbage mixture into a large bowl and stir in salt.
1. Add remaining 1 tbsp sesame oil to pan and heat over medium-high heat.
1. Combine sugar and tofu and add to pan. Cook 3 minutes or until browned, stirring occasionally.
1. Put cabbage mixture into skillet.
1. Stir in hoisin sauce.
1. Set aside.
1. Wet spring roll wraps and spoon about 1/4 to 1/3 cup of the Mu Shu into each spring roll, roll up and enjoy. Serve with hoisin sauce, peanut sauce, or any other desired sauce.
